First-author

  • AI-Driven Forecasting of Vibrio vulnificus in the Southern Baltic Sea Using High-Resolution Data
    Conor Glackin, David Riedinger, Erik Zschaubitz, Lukas Vogel, Theodor Sperlea, Heike Benterbusch, Clara Nietz, and Matthias Labrenz.
    Water Research — Five-year Journal Impact Factor (2024): 12.9).
    Develops a data-driven forecasting framework for V. vulnificus occurrence using high-resolution environmental, microbial, and remote-sensing data. Predictive models include RF, time-lagged RF, and LSTM to classify and forecast pathogen presence as an ML-based early-warning framework for coastal surveillance.
  • Combined TCBS and CHROMagar Analyses Allow for Basic Identification of Vibrio vulnificus within a 48 h Incubation Period in the Coastal Baltic Sea
    Conor Christopher Glackin, Susann Dupke, Thota Sharath Chandra, David Riedinger, and Matthias Labrenz.
    Microorganisms (Five-year Journal Impact Factor (2024): 4.6).
    Evaluates a simple two-step cultivation workflow (TCBS followed by CHROMagar™ Vibrio) for rapid identification of V. vulnificus in coastal waters. Species identification was validated using Vibrio-specific multiplex PCR and sequencing, supporting practical pathogen monitoring in brackish environments.

Co-author

  • Dynamics of an Estuarine Biotic Community Captured in High Spatio-Temporal Resolution Using Metabarcoding
    Theodor Sperlea, Conor Glackin, Lukas Vogel, Erik Zschaubitz, Clara Nietz, Stephanie Elferink, Carina Loose, Henning Schröder, Christiane Hassenrück, and Matthias Labrenz.
    Scientific Data (Nature Portfolio) (Five-year Journal Impact Factor (2024): 8.7).
    Presents a high-resolution spatio-temporal dataset across the Warnow River estuary and adjacent Baltic Sea coast. Surface water was sampled up to twice weekly at fifteen sites along a freshwater–brackish gradient, with 16S/18S amplicon sequencing, flow cytometry, and key environmental parameters recorded to capture fine-scale ecosystem dynamics.
  • High-Resolution Sampling Reveals a Considerable Year-Round Risk Potential for Aquatic Organisms in an Urban Estuary of the Baltic Sea Due to Anthropogenic Pollution Mixture
    Lukas Vogel, Theodor Sperlea, Kathrin Fisch, Conor C. Glackin, Erik Zschaubitz, Detlef E. Schulz-Bull, Matthias Labrenz, and Marion Kanwischer.
    Environmental Pollution (Five-year Journal Impact Factor (2024): 8.1).
    Uses one-year high-resolution monitoring to assess anthropogenic pollution in the Warnow estuary and adjacent Baltic Sea coast. Samples were analysed for 35 target compounds (including pharmaceuticals, pesticides, and UV filters), enabling spatio-temporal analysis of pollutant distributions and evaluation of ecotoxicological risk in a human-impacted estuarine system.
  • A Benchmark Analysis of Feature Selection and Machine Learning Methods for Environmental Metabarcoding Datasets
    Erik Zschaubitz, Henning Schröder, Conor Christopher Glackin, Lukas Vogel, Matthias Labrenz, and Theodor Sperlea.
    Computational and Structural Biotechnology Journal (Five-year Journal Impact Factor (2024): 5.2).
    Benchmarks feature selection and machine-learning workflows across thirteen microbial and metazoan metabarcoding datasets. Compares filter, wrapper, and embedded feature selection approaches combined with classification/regression models, and provides a modular open-source Python framework implementation (Microbiome Machine Learning Benchmark, mbmbm) to support reproducibility.
