About

I am a data scientist with a PhD in environmental microbiology and a strong academic background in mathematics, probability, and statistics. My work focuses on applying machine-learning methods to complex genomic and environmental datasets, with particular expertise in high-resolution time-series analysis and predictive modelling.

I have over two years of industry experience as a Data Scientist at Abbott Laboratories, alongside extensive academic research experience. I am technically proficient in R, JMP, Linux, SQL, and Bash scripting, with hands-on experience using Random Forest and LSTM models, next-generation sequencing workflows, and bioinformatics pipelines.
