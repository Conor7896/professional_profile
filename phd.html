PhD Researcher — Leibniz Institute for Baltic Sea Research (IOW), Rostock, Germany (Feb 2022 – Jan 2026)

Thesis: Quantification, Prediction and Forecasting of Vibrio vulnificus in the Southern Baltic Sea Using High-Resolution Data
Status: Submitted (grade pending)

Research Focus

My role focused on applying statistical and machine-learning methods to environmental biology datasets. I developed and analysed a high-resolution time-series dataset built from weekly sampling and laboratory procedures, with the goal of contributing to an early-warning system for Vibrio vulnificus.

I implemented predictive models (primarily Random Forest and LSTM) and produced results for publication and conference presentations.

Selected Highlights

  • First-author manuscript: AI-Driven Forecasting of Vibrio vulnificus in the Southern Baltic Sea Using High-Resolution Data (submitted to Water Research).
  • Co-author on multiple peer-reviewed papers involving environmental datasets, machine learning evaluation, and marine ecosystem health.
  • Designed and carried out a spatio-temporal sampling campaign across 15 locations in the Baltic Sea and Warnow Estuary.
