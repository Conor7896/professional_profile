Professional Interests

As may be evident from the previous sections, my interests have evolved throughout my academic and professional journey. While studying mathematics as an undergraduate, I quickly discovered that statistics was the area that most appealed to me. Alongside this, I maintained a strong interest in biology and genetics, which naturally led me to pursue a Master’s degree in Computational Genomics. Although this was not the direct path I ultimately followed, it highlighted the importance and power of combining statistical methods with the natural world, and real-world biological systems.

After university, I worked as a Data Scientist in industry, where I gained valuable insight into data-driven decision making, industrial workflows, and the importance of delivering practical and reliable results, particularly within the pharmaceutical sector. My decision to undertake a PhD was driven by a desire to apply these skills in an environmental context, returning to biology with a focus on challenges intensified by climate change. I was especially motivated by the opportunity to combine field sampling, laboratory work, and advanced data analysis within a single research framework.

Approaching the completion of my PhD, my interests have become increasingly focused on programming and data-centric problem solving. The periods I found most rewarding were basically just the times where I was programming, whether it was those spent developing analytical pipelines, models, or workflows. In particular, time-series analysis and predictive modelling have become key areas of interest, and I am keen to further explore their potential across a wider range of applications. I was forecasting when a certain bacteria would be present/absent at locations in the sea and estuary based on data from the previous 5 weeks and this was something I particularly enjoyed. I'm aware artificial intelligence is now a buzzword, but I am genuinely motivated by its ability to solve real-world problems and support evidence-based decision making. Although we are in the early stages, I really think there is an opportunity here to utilise AI to resolve numerous challenges over a broad range of industries, so I would to be in an environment where I can learn, use and develop this. I enjoy working on emerging, interdisciplinary topics, and I hope to continue contributing to innovative, data-driven research in future roles.
